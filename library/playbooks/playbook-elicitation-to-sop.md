# Elicitation to SOP — STUB

**When the fellow runs it:** after discovery, before building. It is the step that turns the discovery
record into the thing the agent executes.

**What it must contain, at minimum:**

1. The transformation, step by step, and it has **three** stages rather than two:
   `interview/discovery-record.md` -> `spec/specification.md` -> `deliverable/skill.md`. Writing
   straight from the record to the deliverable skips the graded artifact.
2. How a heard statement becomes a specified instruction without acquiring precision nobody stated —
   the failure this playbook exists to prevent.
3. The traceability rule: every instruction in the SOP points back at the discovery record, which
   points back at the session [D-28].
4. The handoff to the adversarial-reviewer persona, and what to do with each severity.

**Why the middle stage is not optional.** The specification is the evidence method for nine of the
twenty-five technical competencies, including one whose entire evidence entry reads "the specification
file". The `skill.md` is what the business installs. Two artifacts, two readers, two grading regimes —
the engine reads the first and the owner runs the second.

**The open question, and it is not the author's to settle alone:** whether this is a separate playbook
or part of the interview playbook. The programme record flags it as proposed and undecided. Writing it
as separate is the current assumption, recorded here so the decision is visible rather than implied.
