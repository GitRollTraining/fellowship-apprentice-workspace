# A command failed

Being stuck is normal. Being stuck **quietly** is what costs days.

## Four things, before you ask anyone

**1. Quote the error, all of it.** Not "it says something about permissions". The whole
message, copied out. The line that names the cause is usually not the last line, and summarising
is how it gets thrown away.

**2. Say what you already tried.** Every attempt, including the ones that seemed too obvious to
mention. Otherwise the first thing anyone suggests is the thing you did twenty minutes ago.

**3. Screenshot it if it is not text.** A desktop app, a permission dialog, a browser page: take
the picture and attach it. You can hand a screenshot to your agent and ask what it says. Most
people do not realise this and describe the screen from memory instead.

**4. Say what you expected.** "I expected a version number and got nothing" is a different
problem from "I expected it to finish and it hung", and the two have no fix in common.

## Three things not to do

**Do not retry unchanged.** The same command against the same state gives the same answer. If
nothing between the two runs was different, the second run told you nothing.

**Do not re-prompt with "it didn't work".** An agent given that has less to work with than you
have. Give it the four things above and it can usually name the cause.

**Do not go looking for a private link on the public internet.** Some setup steps need an access
grant that is sent to you directly. If you do not have one, say so and ask your trainer for it by
name.

## Some of these are known and are not your fault

Setup steps fail. Some are expected to. A step that needs a permission only your trainer can give
will stop, and that is the step working, not you failing it.

When you have the four things above and it still will not go, hand them to your trainer and carry
on with something else. **An hour lost on setup is a bug in our material.** Tell us the same day.
